Driving and transport · NSW Photo Card

Apply for a NSW Photo Card

A NSW Photo Card is photo identification for people who don’t have a NSW driver licence. This guide explains what it’s for and how to apply.

Apply with Service NSW ↗
Eligibility: NSW residents aged 16+Application: In personValidity: 5 or 10 years

What is a Photo Card for?

The card shows your photo, name, address and date of birth. It can help you prove your identity, age or address in most places that accept a driver licence.

Some services, including some online applications, may not accept it. Check the specific proof of identity requirements before you rely on it.

How to apply

  1. Check you’re eligible

    You must live in NSW, be at least 16 and provide acceptable proof of identity. You generally can’t also hold a NSW Driver Licence.

  2. Complete the application form

    Download and fill in the NSW Photo Card Application form before your visit.

  3. Visit a Service NSW Centre

    Bring your completed form and required identity documents. Staff will check your application and take your photo.

  4. Pay any fee and receive your card

    Fees depend on your circumstances. Eligible concession card holders may get a free card. Your card is posted to your NSW address.

What to bring

  • Completed application form
  • Current NSW Driver Licence or expired Photo Card, or accepted identity documents and proof of signature
  • Concession card details, if relevant

Before you visit

Check the current document requirements, fees and your nearest service centre on the official site.

If a medical or health condition prevents you from attending, an alternative photo-kit process may be available to eligible people.
